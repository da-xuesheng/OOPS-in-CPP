## Encapsulation

Encapsulation is the process of data-bundling and function-bundling into an unit of class.
It is also used for restricting direct external access to some of the data of the class.

There are 3 types of access specifier.
Access specifier means those controls who can view or modify member data or functions.
1. Private: Those members which are private are only accessible to that class.
2. Public: Those members which are public are accessible from anywhere from outside the class.
3. Protected: Those members which are protected, are accessible in the class and the classes which are child of that class.
